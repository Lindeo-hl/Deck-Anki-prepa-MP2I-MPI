# Deck-Anki-prepa-MP2I-MPI-MPSI-MP

## Français

Voici le deck Anki que deux de mes amis et moi avons construit au cours de la prépa.

Il rassemble la totalité du programme de prépa MP2I et MPI (maths, informatique, physique, anglais, français), ainsi que les connaissances hors programme traitées en cours. Le deck de maths peut être utilisé pour la filière MPSI/MP, qui possède un programme de mathématiques commun avec celui des MP2I/MPI.

Les révisions des cartes Anki ne remplacent pas la relecture du cours, mais elles permettent de fixer les connaissances de manière beaucoup plus durable. Ce travail est difficile, mais votre persévérance finira définitivement par payer. Pour notre part, ce deck Anki nous a apporté une grande tranquillité d'esprit pendant les concours et nous a aidés à intégrer de très bonnes écoles.

Bonne chance dans vos révisions !

**Remarque :** le deck d'anglais est en grande partie tiré d'un deck en ligne déjà existant, créé par un ancien préparationnaire dont je n'ai pas retrouvé la référence précise.

## English

This Anki deck was created by three students during our time in CPGE (Classes Préparatoires aux Grandes Écoles), a highly selective two-year intensive preparatory program in France designed to prepare students for competitive entrance examinations to the country’s top engineering and scientific schools.

The deck covers the complete curriculum of the MP2I and MPI tracks (Mathematics, Physics, Engineering and Computer Science), as well as additional topics covered in class beyond the official syllabus. The mathematics deck can also be used by students in the MPSI/MP tracks, which share a large part of their mathematics curriculum with MP2I/MPI.

Anki flashcards are not a replacement for studying lectures and understanding concepts in depth. However, they are an extremely effective tool for consolidating knowledge over the long term through spaced repetition. Building and reviewing this deck requires significant effort and consistency, but this investment pays off over time.

For us, this deck provided valuable confidence and peace of mind during the competitive entrance examinations. It helped us retain a large amount of information efficiently and contributed to our admission to highly ranked engineering schools.

We hope this deck will be useful to other students preparing for demanding scientific studies.

**Note**: The English vocabulary deck is largely based on an existing online Anki deck created by a former CPGE student. Unfortunately, we were unable to find the exact original source.
